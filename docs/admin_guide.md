# Admin Guide

This guide provides instructions for administrators to manage the e-commerce platform.

## How to Log In

1.  Navigate to the admin login page, typically at `/admin`.
2.  Enter your administrator username and password.
3.  Click the "Log In" button.

## How to Add Products

1.  After logging in, go to the "Products" section in the admin dashboard.
2.  Click on the "Add New Product" button.
3.  Fill in all required product details:
    *   **Product Name:** The title of the product.
    *   **Description:** A detailed description of the product.
    *   **SKU:** Stock Keeping Unit (unique identifier).
    *   **Price:** The selling price of the product.
    *   **Category:** Assign the product to an existing category.
    *   **Stock:** Initial quantity available.
    *   **Condition:** New or Used.
    *   **Images:** Upload product images.
    *   **Brand:** (Optional) Product brand.
    *   **Model Compatibility:** (Optional) Specific vehicle models the product is compatible with.
    *   **Vehicle Year Range:** (Optional) Start and end years for vehicle compatibility.
4.  Click "Save Product" to add it to the inventory.

## How to Edit Products

1.  Go to the "Products" section in the admin dashboard.
2.  Find the product you wish to edit using the search or filter options.
3.  Click on the "Edit" button next to the product.
4.  Modify the necessary product details.
5.  Click "Save Changes" to update the product information.

## How to Manage Stock

1.  Navigate to the "Inventory" section in the admin dashboard.
2.  You can view current stock levels for all products.
3.  To adjust stock for a specific product, find the product and click "Adjust Stock" or similar.
4.  Enter the new stock quantity or the amount to add/remove.
5.  Confirm the changes.

## How to Process Orders

1.  Go to the "Orders" section in the admin dashboard.
2.  View pending orders.
3.  Click on an order to view its details.
4.  Update the order status (e.g., "Processing", "Shipped", "Delivered").
5.  Add tracking information if applicable.
6.  Save the order updates.

## How to Issue Refunds

1.  In the "Orders" section, find the order for which you need to issue a refund.
2.  Click on the order to view details.
3.  Locate the "Refund" or "Issue Refund" option.
4.  Specify the amount to refund (full or partial).
5.  Confirm the refund. This action will typically trigger a refund through the payment gateway (e.g., Stripe).

## How to Moderate Reviews

1.  Go to the "Reviews" section in the admin dashboard.
2.  View submitted customer reviews.
3.  You can approve, edit, or delete reviews that do not meet guidelines.
4.  Ensure reviews are appropriate and helpful for other customers.

## How to Manage Users

1.  Navigate to the "Users" section in the admin dashboard.
2.  View a list of all registered users.
3.  You can:
    *   Edit user profiles (e.g., update contact information).
    *   Change user roles (e.g., promote to admin, demote to customer).
    *   Ban or unban users if necessary.
    *   Reset user passwords (if functionality is available).
